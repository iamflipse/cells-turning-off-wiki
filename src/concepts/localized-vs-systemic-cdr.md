# Localized vs. Systemic CDR & Aging

Like microvascular obstructions, cells turning off in the Cell Danger Response do not have to affect the whole body uniformly—they can lock locally, spread systemically, or sit in a mixed gradient.

## Local Injury Sites as Early Warning Flags

In practice, A Midwestern Doctor emphasizes that **systemic illnesses frequently reveal themselves first inside previously injured regions**:
- Old surgical scars, prior sprains, or poorly healed joint injuries harbor pockets of sensitized, marginally functional tissue.
- Seeing strange localized pain or injury flares immediately after COVID-19 vaccination served clinically as an early predictor that broader systemic CDR issues were brewing.

## Aging as Progressive Biophysical + CDR Accumulation

A Midwestern Doctor closes by tying chronic cellular dormancy directly to normal human aging:
1. Over the decades, declining kidney efficiency and accumulated toxic burdens steadily erode **physiologic zeta potential** and fluid flow.
2. Simultaneously, unresolved micro-shocks leave an ever-larger fraction of cells parked inside a **sustained CDR** across organs.

As a result, many hallmarks traditionally treated as inevitable aging reflect cumulative circulatory stagnation and silent cellular shutdown—which is why therapies designed to restore tissue wakefulness and systemic safety signals become increasingly valuable with age.
