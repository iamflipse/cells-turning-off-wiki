## Core Thesis: Dormant Defense, Not Permanent Cell Death

After a major shock (infection, toxin, interrupted blood flow, physical trauma), tissues often stop performing normal organ work and resist standard integrative or conventional treatments. A Midwestern Doctor argues that in many cases—even conditions labeled irreversible—cells are not dead, but **trapped in an evolutionarily conserved protective shutdown** best mapped by **Dr. Robert K. Naviaux's Cell Danger Response (CDR)**:

```text
  Shock / Trigger ──> Mitochondrial Voltage Drop (electrons diverted)
                                │
                                ▼
  Cells "Turn Off" (CDR1 / CDR2 / CDR3 or dormant hypometabolic lock-in)
     ├── Membrane stiffens, oxygen spikes internally, ATP leaks extracellularly
     ├── Chronic inflammation, lost organ function, or non-linear multi-system illness
     └── Escape requires the 4-Step Reawakening:
           1) Clear primary trigger  ──>  2) Re-awaken dormant cells (circulation / safety)
           3) Address leftovers      ──>  4) Loop back if still blocked
```
