# Key Quotes & Glossary

## Attributed Quotes

### On Metabolomics vs. Static Genomics
> "First, fewer than 2,000 metabolites constitute the majority of the parent molecules in the blood that are used for cell-to-cell communication and metabolism, compared with 6 billion bases in the diploid human genome. Second, metabolites reflect the current functional state of the individual. Collective cellular chemistry represents the functional interaction of genes and environment."
> — **Dr. Robert K. Naviaux**

### On Purines at Every Synapse
> "ATP or its metabolites [have been found to] be co-neurotransmitters and neuromodulators at every synapse in the central and enteric nervous systems, and every immunologic synapse that has been studied to date."
> — **Dr. Robert K. Naviaux**

### On Paradoxical Cellular Reinforcement of Behavior
> "Complex negative and maladaptive behavioral responses to adversity can be paradoxically reinforced by an anti-inflammatory effect they produce at the cellular level."
> — **Dr. Robert K. Naviaux**

### On the Diagnostic Odyssey in Complex Illness
> "Complex [multisystem] diseases like CFS are often difficult and expensive to diagnose [e.g., no single diagnostic test yet exists for CFS]. Although individual tests may be affordable and possibly covered by medical insurance, many patients undergo a diagnostic odyssey that results in substantial personal expenditures that can exceed $100,000 over years of searching [for a cure], absence from the workplace, and significant reductions in quality of life."
> — **Dr. Robert K. Naviaux (2016)**

### On T-Cell Exhaustion
> "[T cell exhaustion] describes a condition in which T cells (a type of immune cell) lose their ability to kill certain cells, such as cancer cells or cells infected with a virus [or mRNA spike proteins]. This can happen when cancer, chronic infection, or other conditions cause the body's immune system to stay active for a long time. Exhausted T cells have high amounts of immune checkpoint proteins on their surface, which may keep the activity of the T cells suppressed..."
> — **National Cancer Institute**, quoted in the essay

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning in This Source |
| --- | --- |
| **Atavistic Survival Model** | Oncology hypothesis arguing cellular stress reactivates ancient unicellular selfish survival genes inside multicellular DNA |
| **Blood Crenation Test** | Older clinical microscopy test observing red-blood-cell shape change in hypertonic fluid, interpreted by the author as detecting CDR-stiffened cell membranes and poor oxygen consumption |
| **Cell Danger Response (CDR)** | Mitochondria-initiated cellular defense shift reducing specialized work, raising intracellular oxygen, stiffening membranes, and leaking danger signals |
| ***Dauer* State** | Evolutionarily conserved hypometabolic survival state seen in Naviaux's 2016 CFS metabolomics: slowed cellular clocks, resilience against starvation, and "brittle" overreaction to minor stimuli |
| **DMSO (Dimethyl Sulfoxide)** | Low-cost compound highlighted by A Midwestern Doctor as an "umbrella" remedy supporting microcirculation and cellular re-awakening in accessible cases |
| **Exosomes** | Intercellular membrane vesicles participating in cell-to-cell CDR signaling (hypothesized by the author to carry spike protein in overloaded cells) |
| **HHV-6** | Human herpesvirus 6 (carried by ~90% by age 3); Naviaux's 2020 study showed it triggers a CDR shielding cells by up to 99% against flu/HSV-1 even after HHV-6 clears |
| **Liquid Crystalline Water** | Structured "fourth phase" intracellular/perimembrane water concentrated with ATP; destructured inside cells during active CDR |
| **Metabolomics** | Mass-spectrometry profiling of `<2,000` core blood metabolites reflecting real-time gene × environment physiology |
| **Mitochondrial Voltage Drop** | Early danger sensor proposed by Naviaux when pathogens or toxins divert electrons away from normal mitochondrial electron transport |
| **Pleomorphism** | Ability of bacteria/fungi under antibiotic or terrain stress to morph into dormant spores or cell-wall-deficient mycoplasma-like forms |
| **Pseudouridine / Pseudouridation** | Nucleotide modification stabilizing synthetic mRNA against degradation; uniquely stayed elevated and climbed over hours after ATP shock in Naviaux's 2021 rat study |
| **Purinergic Signaling** | Danger-alarm network triggered by extracellular ATP (~1,000,000× intracellular concentration) acting on immune, gut, and brain synapses |
| **T-Cell Exhaustion** | Suppression of cytotoxic T cells via surface immune-checkpoint proteins following prolonged infection or antigen exposure, linked clinically to latent EBV reactivation |
| **Zeta Potential** | Electrical repulsive charge keeping particles and blood cells dispersed; collapse causes fluid stagnation that interacts reciprocally with the CDR |
