Why Cells 'Turn Off' & Chronic Disease

Two Root Mechanisms: Circulation & Cells Turning Off

Why can severe acute events—infections, strokes, environmental shocks, or vaccine injuries—leave tissues persistently non-functional long after the initial event seems over?

In clinical practice, A Midwestern Doctor argues these states are routinely misread in two opposite ways: integrative practitioners chase secondary downstream findings (gut dysbiosis, inflammation, mitochondrial markers), while conventional medicine declares the tissue permanently "dead" (such as after a stroke). Both miss a common biological reality: cells often haven't died—they have actively "turned off" into defensive dormancy.

Two Recurring Drivers of Rapid Recovery

Whenever the author observed chronic, debilitating symptoms lift suddenly, the reversal repeatedly traced back to one (or both) of two foundational mechanisms:

  1. Restoring impaired fluid circulation (Physiologic Zeta Potential & Fourth-Phase Water): Clearing microvascular stagnation by restoring electrical charge balance (zeta potential) and structured intracellular water—areas the author notes the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein disrupts strongly. (See CDR, Zeta Potential & Water.)
  2. Turning dormant cells back "on" (Resolving the Cell Danger Response): Clearing the upstream threat (like interrupted blood flow or hidden infection) and delivering the biological safety signal that lets shut-down cells resume normal function.

Clinically, the author cites rapid improvements seen in long-COVID / spike-protein injuries with targeted therapies, plus hundreds of user-reported reversals using DMSO (alongside thousands of studies cited in his broader series).
